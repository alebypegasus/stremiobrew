StremioBrew — a community Stremio client for old rooted LG webOS 4 TVs (the pre-WebAssembly generation the official app dropped).

Work in progress — not widely tested. Supports 15+ languages. webOS 3.x likely will not work; 64-bit TVs will not work; low-RAM models may crash under heavy use.

Requires a rooted TV + Homebrew Channel, a Stremio account, and your own addons (debrid recommended). Ships empty and hosts nothing — it only plays links your addons return. Bundles open-source Node.js + ffmpeg. Not affiliated with Stremio.
