Stremio Lite LG — a community Stremio client for old rooted LG webOS 4 TVs (the pre-WebAssembly generation the official app dropped).

Optimized Edition: Ultra-low RAM & VRAM footprint, DOM virtualization, multi-language internationalization (i18n), and Stremio Theater aesthetics.

Requires a rooted TV + Homebrew Channel, a Stremio account, and your own addons (debrid recommended). Ships empty and hosts nothing — it only plays links your addons return. Bundles open-source Node.js + ffmpeg. Not affiliated with Stremio.
